# Heatwave

## Soil moisture and temperature before heatwaves

[![Soil-moisture and temperature anomalies in a 20-event heatwave composite and the British Columbia case study, shown for three time windows.](figures/Figure1.png)](figures/Figure1.pdf)

**Figure 1. Soil-moisture and temperature anomalies leading up to heatwaves.** The left two columns show a heatwave-centered composite of 20 events; the right two columns show the British Columbia (BC) case study of the 2021 heatwave. Soil-moisture and temperature anomalies are shown in the first and second columns of each pair, respectively. From top to bottom, rows show days -30 to -20, -15 to -10, and -5 to 0 relative to the reference day. Brown and green shading indicate negative and positive soil-moisture anomalies (m³ m⁻³), respectively; blue and red shading indicate negative and positive temperature anomalies (°C). Magenta contours show positive peak temperature anomalies, with negative values shown by dashed blue contours; black contours show 250 hPa height anomalies (m). Shading scales differ between the composite and case-study panels.
